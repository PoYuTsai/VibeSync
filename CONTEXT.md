# VibeSync Interaction Coaching

VibeSync interprets one analyzed interaction at a time while retaining partner-level continuity. The language below separates the current conversational move from the long-term relationship itself.

## Interaction stages

**Analysis fragment**:
One user-selected batch of messages or screenshots that is analyzed as a coherent interaction.
_Avoid_: Whole relationship, permanent transcript

**Stage snapshot**:
The single interaction stage produced by one successful analysis fragment. It describes what this interaction needs now, not the permanent maturity of the relationship.
_Avoid_: Relationship level, rank, unlocked stage

**Latest partner stage**:
The newest valid stage snapshot for one partner, ordered by successful analysis completion time.
_Avoid_: Highest stage, furthest stage

**Unanalyzed partner**:
A partner with no successful valid stage snapshot. This is the only meaning of the question-mark state.
_Avoid_: Unknown stage, analysis failure as opening

**Opening**:
Establishing a reply channel in a genuinely new interaction, or explicitly reconnecting after a material break, silence, or conflict. For an established partner, the visible meaning is reconnecting rather than becoming strangers again. It is never a fallback for missing or malformed stage data or a single short reply.
_Avoid_: Relationship regression, default stage

**Premise**:
Establishing mutual romantic framing, gendered chemistry, playful tension, or personal curiosity beyond ordinary friendly chat.
_Avoid_: Generic warmth, any positive reply

**Qualification**:
Mutually assessing fit through values, boundaries, lifestyle, reciprocity, or relationship expectations.
_Avoid_: Interviewing, one-sided approval seeking

**Narrative**:
Deepening connection through personal stories, emotion, identity, or lived examples that help both people understand each other.
_Avoid_: Catch-all middle stage, any life update

**Close**:
Converting sufficient mutual momentum into a concrete, low-pressure next plan, supported by an invitation window or real scheduling evidence.
_Avoid_: Being a committed couple, user merely wanting a date

**Stage status**:
An orthogonal assessment of whether the current stage is progressing, friend-leaning, ready to advance, or should slow down. It does not choose the partner-card image.
_Avoid_: Interaction stage

**Current interaction focus**:
The user-facing rendering of the latest stage snapshot. It highlights one current task and may move in either direction; it is not a completed-step progress bar.
_Avoid_: Relationship maturity, achievement ladder

**Interaction investment**:
The other person's observable effort in the latest analyzed fragment: substantive content, questions, extensions, callbacks, self-disclosure, emotional specificity, invitation windows, and reciprocity. It does not measure attraction, relationship maturity, or whether continuing is advisable.
_Avoid_: Love score, partner background score, user's message effort

**Relationship context**:
Meeting context, acquaintance duration, current goal, and explicit current facts used to calibrate interpretation. Context never directly awards investment points or chooses a stage without current evidence.
_Avoid_: Stage command, score modifier

**Partner memory chips**:
User-confirmed background about the partner that may tailor timing, channel, topic, pacing, and feasible plans. These facts are not quotes from the current conversation and never excuse current low investment.
_Avoid_: Current-message evidence, automatic stage or score

**About Me context**:
The user's current blockers, coaching goals, boundaries, and saved topic profile. In the current product contract it can support Coach 1:1 but is excluded from AnalyzeChat stage, investment, and five-style classification.
_Avoid_: Partner fact, stage input, investment input

**Stage continuity**:
The previous valid partner-scoped stage used as a weak prior for a later fragment. Strong current evidence may advance, skip, or retreat; ambiguous evidence must not cause oscillation or default to Opening.
_Avoid_: Monotonic floor, highest-ever stage
