# VibeSync Interaction Coaching

VibeSync interprets one analyzed interaction at a time while retaining partner-level continuity. The language below separates the current conversational move from the long-term relationship itself.

## Interaction stages

**Analysis fragment**:
One user-selected batch of messages or screenshots that is analyzed as a coherent interaction.
_Avoid_: Whole relationship, permanent transcript

**Stage snapshot**:
The single interaction stage produced by one successful analysis fragment. It describes what this interaction needs now, not the permanent maturity of the relationship.
_Avoid_: Relationship level, rank, unlocked stage

**Latest partner stage**:
The newest valid stage snapshot for one partner, ordered by successful analysis completion time.
_Avoid_: Highest stage, furthest stage

**Unanalyzed partner**:
A partner with no successful valid stage snapshot. This is the only meaning of the question-mark state.
_Avoid_: Unknown stage, analysis failure as opening

**Opening**:
Establishing a reply channel in a genuinely new interaction, or explicitly reconnecting after a material break. It is never a fallback for missing or malformed stage data.
_Avoid_: Relationship regression, default stage

**Premise**:
Establishing mutual romantic framing, gendered chemistry, playful tension, or personal curiosity beyond ordinary friendly chat.
_Avoid_: Generic warmth, any positive reply

**Qualification**:
Mutually assessing fit through values, boundaries, lifestyle, reciprocity, or relationship expectations.
_Avoid_: Interviewing, one-sided approval seeking

**Narrative**:
Deepening connection through personal stories, emotion, identity, or lived examples that help both people understand each other.
_Avoid_: Catch-all middle stage, any life update

**Close**:
Converting sufficient mutual momentum into a concrete, low-pressure next plan, supported by an invitation window or real scheduling evidence.
_Avoid_: Being a committed couple, user merely wanting a date

**Stage status**:
An orthogonal assessment of whether the current stage is progressing, friend-leaning, ready to advance, or should slow down. It does not choose the partner-card image.
_Avoid_: Interaction stage
